Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Overview

OceanArt is an AI-powered application that allows users to generate videos from photos, text prompts, or creative inputs. OceanArt provides pre-made templates and is designed to minimize the personal data we collect.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 User-Uploaded Content

Images, prompts, and template selections you provide in order to generate a video.

2.2 Generated Output

The videos produced by our AI generation service based on your input.

2.3 Device & Diagnostic Data

Basic device information and crash/diagnostic data used to keep the app stable and improve performance.

Uploaded images and prompts are automatically deleted after processing. Processing typically takes approximately 60 seconds.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information above solely to operate and improve OceanArt — generating your video, maintaining app stability, and providing customer support. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Retention

Uploaded content used for generation is deleted after your video is produced. Generated videos are stored on your device unless you choose to save or share them elsewhere. Account and subscription records are retained only as long as necessary to provide the service.

5. Third-Party Services

OceanArt relies on trusted third-party providers to operate the app, including:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, analytics, crash reporting, and cloud infrastructure
  • RevenueCat — subscription and in-app purchase management
  • Apple App Store — payment processing for subscriptions and purchases

These providers process data under their own privacy policies and only to the extent necessary to provide their service to OceanArt.

6. Children's Privacy

OceanArt is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to maintain appropriate safeguards.

8. Your Choices

You can control camera and photo library permissions through your device settings at any time. You may also request account or data deletion by contacting us through our Support page.

9. Content Restrictions

OceanArt does not permit the generation of illegal, infringing, or harmful content. See our Terms of Service for details.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Reach out via our Support page.
