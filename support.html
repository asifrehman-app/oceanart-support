Frequently Asked

How do I restore a purchase?

Open OceanArt → Settings → tap Restore on the Pro screen. Purchases restore automatically to the Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → OceanArt to cancel or change your plan.

My video didn't generate — what happened?

Generation can occasionally fail due to network issues or unsupported input. Your credits are not deducted for failed generations — try again, and contact us if the issue continues.
