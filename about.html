About OceanArt

OceanArt is an AI-powered video creation app designed to help you turn photos, text prompts, and creative ideas into engaging videos — quickly, and without any editing experience required.

What OceanArt Does

OceanArt combines AI generation with a simple, template-driven workflow. Instead of learning complex editing software, you pick a template, provide an image or a prompt, and let the AI handle the rest.

Key Features

AI Video GenerationCreate videos from a single photo or prompt
Photo → VideoTransform a still image into motion
Text → VideoDescribe what you want, AI generates it
Ready-Made TemplatesTrending styles across dance, travel, luxury & more
Visual EffectsPolished, share-ready output
Simple InterfaceNo editing skills needed

Designed For Creators

OceanArt is built for creators, social media users, and anyone who wants great-looking videos without the time investment of traditional editing tools.

How It Works

  • Choose a template that fits the video you want to create
  • Upload a photo and/or enter a text prompt
  • OceanArt generates your video
  • Preview, save, and share
